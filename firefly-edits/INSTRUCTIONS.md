# Firefly edits (firefly.adobe.com, then Generative Fill)

For each image: upload it, brush ONLY the area listed, paste the prompt, and Generate. Pick the best result and download it into this folder with `-edited` added to the name. Then tell Claude and it will stage the result on the preview.

Tip: keep the brushed area tight. Anything outside the brush stays exactly as it is.

## Images 1–4: Fresh Beauty steps (original photos)
Goal: the same eyebrow on all four, and the N logo on the applicator placed like the real one (references/real-applicator-closeup.png).

**Eyebrow.** Use image 4 as the look to match: a soft low arch, medium thickness, thin tail, soft dark brown.
- Brush: only the eyebrow (cards 1, 2 and 3).
- Prompt: `natural soft dark brown eyebrow, soft low arch, medium thickness tapering to a thin tail, feathery individual hairs`

**Applicator N logo** (all four images, one at a time):
1. Brush the existing small N on the handle, and Generate with the prompt `plain polished silver metal` to erase it.
2. Brush a small spot on the wide part of the handle, about one logo-width before it narrows toward the sponge.
3. Prompt: `small thin black serif letter N logo printed on polished silver metal`.
4. Compare with references/real-applicator-closeup.png. If the N comes out the wrong way round, generate again.
