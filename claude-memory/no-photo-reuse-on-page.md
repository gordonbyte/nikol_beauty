On a coming-soon page, every slot gets a different photo. When a new slot needs an image (or Randell asks to swap one), choose from the supplied photos that are NOT yet on the page — even if it needs text removal or a harder crop — rather than re-cropping a photo already used in another section.

**Why:** 2026-09-30, WaterProof Eyeliner: I put the row-3 barrels photo into the skin-safe slot too (Randell had asked for that one, but I also suggested another reuse). He: "why are you reusing photos when we have enough not to reuse?" and pointed at the unused `1 - Eyeliner-01` (text to remove).

**How to apply:** before proposing any image, list which supplied photos are already on the page and which are unused; propose from the unused set first. If the requested image is already used elsewhere on the page, say so and offer an unused alternative. Also avoid near-duplicates (same shoot/eye in two slots) — flag them.
