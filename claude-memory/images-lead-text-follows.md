Randell, 2026-10-06: "you can change the text content to better match the images we are going to be generating moving forward … if you know the face is going to look better doing a certain pose vs one to fit what was written in the text, I do not want the system to force itself to fit prewritten text and sacrifice quality."

**Why:** forcing poses to match existing copy cost quality in earlier rounds. Two examples: step 1 "Line and Fill First" kept asking for a liner while Randell wanted the lipstick, and some poses made faces and hands worse.

**How to apply:**
- Choose the pose or shot that will look best and most accurate, guided by the playbook ([[coming-soon-image-playbook]]).
- Then propose updated titles and text for that slot (step title and text, row title and text, banner heading) to match.
- Stage the text changes together with the image. Don't change copy unrelated to new images.
- Accuracy still wins over creativity: correct product and real-looking face.
