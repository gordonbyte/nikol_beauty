In product images, the product must look brand new: smooth, freshly pressed pans with no dents, dips, swirl marks, loose powder or fallout. An applicator or brush must never be shown touching or dipping into the eyeshadow. It can be held above the palette, laid beside it or sit in its tray.

**Why:** 2026-10-06, Strong Brew row 1. Randell: "i do not want the product to look used" and "i do not want the brush in the makup".

**How to apply:**
- Write "brand new, untouched pans, applicator not touching the pans" into every product prompt.
- Swatch shots (swipes on a surface) are a separate thing and still OK, but keep the palette's own pans pristine in them.
- Images staged before this rule that show used product (Naughty Nancy row 2 with the applicator in the pan, Le Bottled Blonde row 2's crushed pan) were flagged to Randell.
- Part of [[coming-soon-image-playbook]].
