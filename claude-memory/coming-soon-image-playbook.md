Drafted 2026-10-06 at Randell's request. It's awaiting his approval and edits. The source is `changelog/coming-soon-image-playbook.md` in the repo; the published copy is https://claude.ai/artifact/NnJkByaEKx7i5FpS9smJLB.

It covers:
- **The 4 image groups per page:**
  - Steps (3, or 4 on palettes): teach
  - Skin-safe (1): reassure
  - Why-switched rows (2–3): prove the claim
  - Banner (1): convert
- **Shot library S1–S10**, the only shots we use: hand, hero on stone or bathroom, application, next to face, mirror reflection, texture smudge, face smudge, finished result, pairing flat lay, shade row.
- **Tools:**
  - Nano Banana Pro with product and Nikol elements is the default.
  - GPT Image 2.5 when lettering matters.
  - Full faces only from real photos or the Soul.
  - NB2 mask inpainting for all fixes, never whole-image edits.
  - FLUX.2 Outpaint for crops.
- **Order per product:** setup → skin-safe → row smudge → steps → rows → banner. One image at a time.

Re-read the repo file before starting any product; Randell may have edited it. Related: [[one-image-at-a-time]], [[higgsfield-account-inventory]].
