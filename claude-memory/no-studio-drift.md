Randell, 2026-10-09, on the first Perfect Complexion set: "these were not that good please take your time and do better". That set was speed-batched: studio flat lays on linen or marble, the same back-of-hand swatch idea in several slots, and wrong packaging (plain Eye Primer compact, no pump on the primer bottle).

**Why:** speed-batching whole pages had drifted away from [[coming-soon-image-style]] and [[coming-soon-image-playbook]].

**How to apply:**
- Before generating a page, pull the real product photos and check the packaging details: lids, pumps, tube colour, caps. Pull real shade names too.
- Use the 35mm sunlit style block and varied real settings (bathroom, café, car, bedroom window). Keep one setting for all the Steps.
- Use anonymous partial-face shots (from mid-nose down) for application.
- Review every image at full size before staging, then fix with edits or crops.
- At most one plain flat lay per page.
