## Local staging for new coming-soon images (in `shopify-live/`, per Randell)
New images and text are staged locally first and pushed to the dev store all at once at the end.
- `assets/custom-coming-soon-staging.json` lists staged content by product handle: skinsafe, rows 1–3,
  steps 1–4, banner. Each entry has src, alt, an optional title/text/heading, and a status.
- `snippets/custom-cs-staging.liquid` is the loader. It runs only on localhost / 127.0.0.1 (the theme dev
  preview), fetches the JSON through the local `/cdn/...` path (the dev-store CDN URL is a different origin
  the local page can't read), and swaps the staged images and text into the 4 sections. It finds them by
  their existing class names, so the 4 section files were not edited. It is inert on the dev or live domain.
- `sections/custom-product-coming-soon.liquid` gets one line, `render 'custom-cs-staging'`, after the
  cart-bar block and outside its condition.
- Tested on 127.0.0.1:9292/products/rose-lip-balm: a temporary entry swapped the skin-safe and step 1 images
  and the step 1 title. The test entry was removed.
- Not mirrored to `shopify-dev/` (Randell said shopify-live).
