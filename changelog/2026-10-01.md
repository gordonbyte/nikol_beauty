## Lipsticks — the two-tone lips fixed, all 40 repainted images redone (DEV)

- Randell on 1975: "the lips look like 2 colors in certain areas like the edge of the smiles".
  He was right, and it was a real flaw in the mask rather than a tuning miss.
- **Cause: the gate tested absolute redness, which scales with brightness.** Measured at the smile
  corner the pixel is 82/32/31 — unmistakably lipstick at 0.62 saturation — but its absolute
  redness R-(G+B)/2 is only 51 against a threshold of 95. So every crease, lip line and shadowed
  corner failed the test and kept the original colour while the lit centre of the lip changed.
  Two colours on one mouth, worst where the shadow is deepest, which is exactly the edge of a
  smile.
- **Fix: divide redness by R.** That removes the brightness term and the separation holds at any
  lightness — inside the lip box skin tops out near 0.46 and lipstick starts at 0.62, dark corners
  included. Teeth sit at 0.15 and are never touched.
- **Membership instead of a yes/no gate.** Weight now ramps from 0 at 0.48 to 1 at 0.60, so the
  genuinely ambiguous band between lip and skin gets partial weight and blends, rather than
  switching hard at one threshold. Source statistics come only from pixels above 0.6, so the
  half-weight edge pixels do not drag the mean.
- **Chroma now moves multiplicatively and hue rotates**, where before a and b shifted by a fixed
  amount. A fixed shift over-colours dark pixels, which carry little chroma of their own; scaling
  keeps a crease reading as a crease of that shade. Lightness handling is unchanged.
- Verified on the pixel that failed: the corner at (1007,185) in the banner read 82/32/33 before
  and 97/60/54 after — a dark terracotta instead of untouched red. Checked the lit lip too, which
  barely moves, as it should.
- All 40 repainted images redone (5 slots x 8 shades) and replaced in place. The 16 step2/step3
  crops are untouched photography and needed no change.
- **Watch out:** fetching a Files CDN URL without its `?v=` param after a `fileUpdate` returned
  the pre-update image and made the fix look like it had not applied. The versioned URL from the
  API had the new bytes, and the storefront was correct all along since Liquid emits the version.
  Noted in the playbook.

### Gloss repaint: the original colour was still showing at the product edges — remasked

Randell: "do better with checking the edges of each product. i am still seeing colors form the
original." He was right, and measuring found two separate faults.

**Fault 1 — the brightness floor was cutting the tube's own rim.** The old mask was one per-pixel
rule: normalised redness over 0.48 plus a brightness floor of R 100 to hold the burgundy leather
jacket out. Profiling across the tube edge showed the two cannot be separated that way: the tube's
dark rim runs down to R 92 while the jacket runs up to R 97. The floor that excluded the jacket
also excluded the tube's rim, which therefore kept the original brick colour.

**Fault 2 — it was worst exactly where it showed most.** Measured through the repaint gate, the
Champs tube read 113/59/47 against a 239/157/140 target, from only 369 surviving pixels — the
measurement was finding nothing but the leftovers. Upgrade, whose target happens to sit near the
source in lightness, measured 221/89/96 against 226/93/101 and looked fine. So the paler the
shade, the more obvious the unrepainted rim.

The fix separates the two jobs the old rule was trying to do at once:

- **Colour says "this is product".** Normalised redness with *no* brightness floor, so the dark rim
  comes in with the rest of the tube.
- **Shape says "this is the object".** The tube/jacket boundary is traced as a polygon — read off
  pixel profiles across the tube at three heights, since the edges are straight on a rigid object —
  and inside that outline everything is repainted except her fingernails, which are the one bright
  low-chroma thing crossing it. Specular highlights are deliberately included: a chroma multiply on
  a near-neutral pixel does nothing, and excluding them split the mask into slivers.
- For the lips and the wand, where colour alone does separate cleanly, the mask keeps the colour
  rule and adds **opening by reconstruction** — erode to snap the thin necks that let shadowed skin
  in, keep the object's blob, then dilate back inside the original mask so the rim is not rounded
  off the way a plain opening would.
- Colour statistics are still taken only over the saturated core of the mask. The mask now includes
  highlights on purpose, and averaging those in would read the source as pale and drive the whole
  tube dark to compensate.
- The traced outline has to stop where her fingers take over; at a 2px feather that cut read as a
  faint straight edge across the tube, so tube parts feather at 7px.

Two things tried and dropped:
- Seeding the reconstruction inside the tube instead of taking the largest blob. The tube is a
  narrow diagonal strip, so erosion favours the chunkier finger shadow and the largest-blob rule
  picked the wrong object — but seeding then landed in a sliver split off by a specular highlight.
  Moot once the polygon made the component search unnecessary.
- An outline for the tube's lower end where it shows between her fingers. Rendered, it was mostly
  finger: a coloured block across her knuckle. A small dark area that reads as shadow beats a wrong
  one that reads as a sticker, so it was removed.
