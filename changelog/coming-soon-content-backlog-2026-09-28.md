# Coming-soon page content — what is still to do

Audited 2026-09-28 against the DEV store (nikolbeauty-dev-mab1srre). Source of truth: the
`coming_soon_page` and `cs_content_item` metaobjects, read in full — 70 products, 66 page
entries, 750 content cards.

**How "done" is judged.** A page counts as done when its step squares hold real photographs.
Every unfinished page below already has its three step cards written (title and body copy),
its Skin-Safe heading, a real big Skin-Safe image, three filled badge slots, three "why they
switched" rows and its FAQs. The one thing missing is the step photography: all three squares
still point at the shared placeholder file (MediaImage/31441077633126).

Caveat: "real" means "not the placeholder file". Individual images have not been eyeballed for
quality, and some big Skin-Safe images are packshots rather than model photography.

## Handle mismatches worth knowing

Two page entries do not match their product handle, so a handle-based lookup misses them:

- page `bb-creme` ↔ product `bb-cream`
- page `soft-set-technique-brush` ↔ product `soft-set-technique™-brush` (the product handle
  contains a ™)

Both pages do exist and are wired up; only an automated match by handle would miss them.
